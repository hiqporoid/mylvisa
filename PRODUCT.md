# Mylvisa product direction

Mylvisa is a Finnish daily rarity quiz for broad general-knowledge players. Its purpose is to turn one known answer into a satisfying choice: every accepted member is correct, while less obvious recall earns more points.

The experience should feel witty, restrained and confident rather than academic, childish or crowded. The core gameplay screen is a game stage, not a document. One primary decision is visible at a time, feedback is humane, and motion explains progress.

Design principles:

1. Every prompt is immediately understandable and offers a familiar entry answer plus a meaningful long tail.
2. Invalid input costs only time. It never shames the player, reveals the answer universe or silently ends a round.
3. Canonicalisation resolves a concept the player already identified; it never becomes autocomplete or a hint oracle.
4. Every accepted answer visibly advances the original Mylvintäaalto world.
5. Dark off-white and acid-lime remain the identity. Avoid underwater metaphors, copied trade dress, excessive cards, decorative motion and juvenile illustration.
6. Keyboard, focus, contrast, safe areas and reduced-motion semantics are product requirements, not polish.
